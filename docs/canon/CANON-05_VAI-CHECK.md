# 0 — ⚠️ WHAT IT IS

**The gate. The system of truth.**

⚠️⚠️ **NOTHING VAIRIFY OFFERS IS VALID WITHOUT IT. FREE. PERMANENTLY.**

## 0.1 — ⚠️ Why it is the system of truth

**The people are there.** ⚠️ **Every other check in the product is a machine's opinion. This
one happens with two people in the same room who can look at each other.**

⚠️⚠️ **THAT IS ALSO WHY LIVENESS IS NOT A CRISIS HERE.** **If he holds a photograph to the
camera, she sees him do it.** ⚠️ **The witness is the check.**

---

# 1 — ⚠️⚠️ ONE RULE. TWO TIMINGS.

> ⚠️⚠️ **"IT'S ONE RULE, AND THEY BOTH WORK."**

**Every V.A.I. check is the same four things in the same order:**

```
1  ⚠️ AN EVENT IS CREATED
        ↓
2  ⚠️⚠️ THE EVENT CREATES A QR CODE
        ↓
3  ⚠️ BOTH PARTIES ACCEPT OR DENY
        ↓        ⚠️⚠️ ACCEPTANCE SIGNS THE MUTUAL CONSENT CONTRACT
4  ⚠️⚠️ BOTH SCAN THEIR FACES
        ↓
   ⚠️ IT IS A MEETING
```

⚠️⚠️ **WHAT DIFFERS BETWEEN PATHS IS ONLY WHEN STEPS 1 TO 3 HAPPEN. THE SEQUENCE NEVER
CHANGES.**

## 1.1 — ⚠️⚠️ TWO THINGS ARE REQUIRED AND NEITHER SUBSTITUTES FOR THE OTHER

> ⚠️ **"You need both. The face binding makes it official. If you accept and never scan, it
> doesn't work. If you scan and then accept, that doesn't work."**

| ❌ | |
|---|---|
| **Accept, never scan** | ⚠️⚠️ **NOT A MEETING** |
| **Scan, then accept** | ⚠️⚠️ **NOT A MEETING. THE ORDER IS PART OF THE RULE.** |

⚠️ **Accept binds the agreement. The face binds the person to it.**

## 1.2 — ⚠️⚠️ HISTORY IS NEVER REVEALED

> ⚠️ **"History is never shown. Accessed at any time."**

⚠️⚠️ **THERE IS NO REVEAL MOMENT ANYWHERE IN THIS FLOW.**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️ **History is available at any time, to anyone holding the number.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **SO THE DOOR IS NOT WHERE SHE FINDS OUT.** **She looked when she wanted to.** |
| 3 | ❌ **No "reviews reveal" step. Not on either path.** |

⚠️ **This removes the load-bearing problem in `CANON-05` §5 entirely.** **Path B was only
safe if the booking screen showed his history — now nothing depends on any screen showing
it, because it is always reachable.**

# 2 — ⚠️ PATH A — IN PERSON

**No prior arrangement. They are standing together.**

```
1  ⚠️ ONE OF THEM STARTS A V.A.I. CHECK
        ↓
2  ⚠️⚠️ AN EVENT IS CREATED. THE EVENT CREATES A QR.
        ↓
3  ⚠️ EITHER DISPLAYS IT. THE OTHER SCANS.
        ↓        ⚠️⚠️ SYMMETRIC — §4
4  ⚠️ BOTH SEE WHATEVER THEY CAN SEE ABOUT THE OTHER
        ↓
5  ⚠️ BOTH ACCEPT OR DENY
        ↓        ⚠️⚠️ ACCEPTING SIGNS THE CONTRACT
6  ⚠️⚠️ BOTH SCAN THEIR FACES
        ↓
7  ⚠️ IT IS A MEETING
```

⚠️ **Nothing has happened until step 5. A person can walk away at any point before it and
nothing exists.**

# 3 — ⚠️⚠️ PATH B — PREARRANGED

> ⚠️ **"The exact same sequence is happening, just at a different time."**

## 3.1 — ⚠️ Online, days earlier

```
1  ⚠️ HE SENDS A REQUEST
        ↓        ⚠️⚠️ IT IS A REQUEST. NOT AN EVENT.
2  ⚠️ SHE ACCEPTS
        ↓
3  ⚠️⚠️ BOTH PARTIES HAVE NOW ACCEPTED
        ↓        ⚠️ THE CONTRACT IS SIGNED
4  ⚠️⚠️ ONLY NOW DOES IT BECOME AN EVENT
        ↓
5  ⚠️ IT APPEARS IN THE PREARRANGED PANEL
```

> ⚠️⚠️ **"THE ONLINE ONLY BECOMES AN EVENT ONCE IT'S COMPLETED." "BOTH PARTIES MUST
> ACCEPT."**

⚠️ **A request accepted by one side is still a request.** **It is not in the panel, it has no
QR, and nothing is booked.**

## 3.2 — ⚠️⚠️ ACCEPTANCE ONLINE IS THE QR SCAN

> ⚠️ **"When they accept, they don't have to scan the QR code. Acceptance of an online
> protocol IS the QR code — they've accepted online."**

⚠️⚠️ **STEPS 1 THROUGH 3 OF THE ONE RULE ARE ALREADY DONE BEFORE THEY MEET.**

## 3.3 — ⚠️ At the door

```
1  ⚠️ THE PREARRANGED PANEL — she taps the item
        ↓
2  ⚠️⚠️ SCAN THE QR
        ↓        ⚠️ TO CONFIRM THE SAME TWO PEOPLE ARE PRESENT
        ↓          WHO MADE THE ARRANGEMENT
3  ⚠️⚠️ BOTH SCAN THEIR FACES
        ↓
4  ⚠️ IT IS A MEETING
```

⚠️ **Two things remain at the door and that is all.**

⚠️⚠️ **THE QR SCAN HERE IS NOT AN AGREEMENT. IT IS A PRESENCE CHECK** — the same two
credentials that agreed online are the two credentials here now.

❌ **No accept step. It was made.** ❌ **No contract signature. It was signed.** ❌ **No
history reveal.** — §1.2

# 4 — ⚠️⚠️ THE QR IS SYMMETRIC

> ⚠️ **"One code. You can scan or you can be scanned. It's still the same event."**

| # | Why |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **THE PROTOCOL MUST NOT ENCODE WHO IS PROVIDER AND WHO IS CLIENT.** **Fix the direction and the mechanism starts assuming roles.** ⚠️ **Everything else in this product is deliberately symmetrical — no-show, review gates, DateGuard.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **One dead camera, one cracked screen, one phone in bright sun.** **If only one direction works, the meeting stalls at the door over something trivial.** |

⚠️ **The QR is unique to THIS event.** ⚠️⚠️ **A STATIC PERSONAL QR CAN BE SCREENSHOTTED AND
REUSED. THIS IS A ONE-TIME TOKEN.**

# 5 — ⚠️ THE FACE SCAN

## 5.1 — ⚠️⚠️ HERE, AND ONLY HERE, THERE ARE THREE BANDS

⚠️ **Login and registration are pass or no pass** — `DESIGN-BRIEF-26` §1.3.

⚠️⚠️ **VAI-CHECK IS THE ONE PLACE WITH GREEN, YELLOW AND RED, BECAUSE SHE HAS TO DECIDE
ABOUT SOMEONE STANDING IN FRONT OF HER.**

| Band | ⚠️ What she sees |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **GREEN** | **It is him. Nothing announced.** |
| ⚠️ **YELLOW** | ⚠️ **Not confident. Manual verification is available. Proceed with caution.** |
| ⚠️⚠️ **RED** | ⚠️ **Not this person. Extreme caution.** ⚠️⚠️ **RED IS WHAT TRIGGERS MANUAL.** |

⚠️⚠️ **NEVER A PERCENTAGE. THE ARITHMETIC STAYS AT CHAINPASS.**

⚠️ **The thresholds are global, set in ChainPass admin, adjustable without a deploy.**
⚠️⚠️ **DRAW THEM AS CONFIGURED VALUES. DO NOT HARDCODE A NUMBER ON A SCREEN.**

## 5.2 — ⚠️ Yellow and red must not sound the same

⚠️⚠️ **AT RED SHE READS THAT PHOTOGRAPH WITH FAR MORE SCRUTINY THAN AT YELLOW, AND THE COPY
IS WHAT TELLS HER TO.**

⚠️ **`End this` is the first button in both.** ⚠️⚠️ **THE MOMENT PROCEED LOOKS LIKE THE
DEFAULT, THE SCREEN IS REASSURING AGAIN.**

# 6 — ⚠️⚠️ MANUAL — IN PERSON ONLY

> ⚠️ **"The only time a manual is going to be in person. You need to spend the five minutes
> and get a new picture."**

## 6.1 — Why

⚠️ **In person the photograph does real work — she compares a picture to a face in front of
her.**

⚠️⚠️ **REMOTE, THERE IS NOTHING TO COMPARE AGAINST.** **She would be judging two photographs
of a person who is not there, making a match she is not equipped to make, and carrying the
consequence.**

## 6.2 — The flow

```
⚠️ RED OR YELLOW
        ↓
⚠️ SHE TAPS VERIFY MANUALLY
        ↓
⚠️ HE IS ASKED TO ACCEPT
        ↓        ⚠️ refusing costs him nothing on record — but she is told
⚠️⚠️ CHAINPASS SERVES HIS VERIFICATION PHOTOGRAPH TO HER SCREEN
        ↓
⚠️⚠️ SHE LOOKS AT THE PHOTO. SHE LOOKS AT HIM. SHE DECIDES.
```

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **NOBODY AT EITHER COMPANY LOOKS AT ANYTHING.** **ChainPass serves the file.** ❌ **No queue, no wait state, no shift, no reviewer.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **VAIRIFY RENDERS AND NEVER STORES.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **She can screenshot it.** **Anything the screen says about visibility is a STATEMENT, not a control. Word it accordingly.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **Manual is a Pro feature.** **ChainPass sets it. A platform opts in by API.** |

## 6.3 — ⚠️ Remote failure has no manual

⚠️ **He is at home and his own check fails.**

⚠️⚠️ **HE IS TOLD HIS OWN RESULT AND ROUTED TO RE-VERIFY. THAT IS ALL.**

> **Your check did not pass.**
> **Re-verify at ChainPass — it takes a few minutes.**

⚠️ **Routed exactly like a login failure.** ⚠️ **Re-verification is NOT a signup** — no
account, no disclosure, no screening, no new V.A.I. **It replaces the photograph.**

# 8 — ⚠️ THE RE-CHECK

⚠️ **Either party, any time, mid-encounter. Same mechanic: QR, both faces.**

**Why:** the door check confirmed identity at the door. ⚠️⚠️ **IT SAYS NOTHING ABOUT WHO IS
IN THE ROOM AN HOUR LATER.**

## 8.1 — ⚠️⚠️ DENIABILITY MATTERS MORE HERE THAN AT THE DOOR

⚠️ **At the door, "verify me" is procedure. Mid-encounter it is an accusation — unless it is
easy to hit and impossible to explain away.**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️ **Easy to hit.** ⚠️⚠️ **"OH, I HIT THAT BY ACCIDENT" MUST REMAIN A COMPLETE, SOCIALLY NORMAL EXCUSE.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **Impossible to cancel from her side.** **Once triggered it does not clear until he scans.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **Identical whether deliberate or accidental.** ❌ **No confirmation step.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **Neutral wording** — *Verification requested* · *Scan to continue.* ❌ **Nothing that reads as suspicion.** |
| 5 | ⚠️ **Does NOT restart the review clock.** **Otherwise the window is extendable at will.** |

⚠️ **Where it lives: reachable without navigating.** **If she is mid-encounter, DateGuard is
likely armed and the Safety button is already showing state.**
