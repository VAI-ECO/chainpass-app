# 1 — ⚠️⚠️ EVERY BINDING ACTION IS SEALED WITH A FACE SCAN

> ⚠️⚠️ **"ALL BINDING OFFERS AND ALL BINDING ACTIONS ARE SEALED WITH A FACIAL SCAN."**

## 1.1 — ⚠️ The test

**Ask one question: does this bind someone?**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **IT BINDS** | ⚠️⚠️ **IT SCANS.** |
| ⚠️ **IT DOES NOT BIND** | ⚠️⚠️ **IT DOES NOT SCAN.** |

> ⚠️ **"We don't want it to be cumbersome."**

⚠️⚠️ **A SCAN THAT SEALS NOTHING IS FRICTION FOR ITS OWN SAKE. REMOVE IT.**

## 1.2 — ⚠️ The one exception

⚠️ **Entry.** **Not because it binds, but because the credential has to be established
before anything else can happen.**

## 1.3 — ⚠️ Worked example — the invitation

⚠️ **This is why an invitation has exactly two scans and not four.**

| # | | Binds? | Scan? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **He writes an invitation** | ❌ **An offer to the world** | **No** |
| 2 | **She sends her profile back, with or without a different price** | ❌ ⚠️ **HE MAY RECEIVE MANY. NONE OF THEM BIND HIM.** | **No** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **He accepts one** | ⚠️⚠️ **BINDING** | ⚠️ **YES** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **She accepts back** | ⚠️⚠️ **BINDING. IT IS NOW AN EVENT.** | ⚠️ **YES** |

⚠️⚠️ **STEP 2 REQUIRES NO SCAN PRECISELY BECAUSE IT BINDS NOBODY. THAT IS WHY SHE CAN SEND
MANY.**

## 1.4 — ⚠️ The inversion

| | Who elicits | Who binds first | Who closes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ **VAIRIDATE** | **He proposes** | **He does, by sending** | ⚠️ **She has the last word** |
| ⚠️ **INVITATION** | ⚠️⚠️ **HE ELICITS** | ⚠️⚠️ **HE DOES, BY ACCEPTING ONE** | ⚠️ **She closes it** |

⚠️ **Same sequence. Reversed.**

# 2 — ⚠️⚠️ AGREEING TO A VAI-CHECK CLOSES EVERY COMPETING OFFER

> ⚠️ **"Any time a V.A.I. check is agreed to, all other current actions are gone."**

## 2.1 — ⚠️⚠️ WHY — AND THE REASON IS THE BOUNDARY

⚠️⚠️ **SHE IS ONE PERSON, IN ONE PLACE, AT ONE TIME.**

**So the rule closes anything that would put her in two places, and nothing else.**

| ⚠️ **CLOSES** | ⚠️ **SURVIVES** |
|---|---|
| ⚠️⚠️ **EVERY OUTSTANDING OFFER THAT OVERLAPS THE AGREED WINDOW** | ⚠️ **Anything for a different time** |
| ⚠️ **Available Now, if the agreement is for now** | ⚠️⚠️ **AN ALREADY-CONFIRMED EVENT FOR ANOTHER TIME** |
| **Her outstanding invitation responses for that window** | **Her invitation responses for other windows** |
| **His other invitation acceptances for that window** | |

⚠️⚠️ **A CONFIRMED THURSDAY VAIRIDATE DOES NOT CLOSE BECAUSE SHE AGREED TO SOMETHING
TONIGHT. IT DOES NOT CONFLICT.**

⚠️ **An offer is not an event.** ⚠️⚠️ **THIS RULE CLOSES OFFERS. IT NEVER CANCELS A
CONFIRMED EVENT.** **Cancelling is a separate, deliberate act by a person** — `CANON-PL-08` §9.

## 2.2 — ⚠️ It applies to both parties

⚠️⚠️ **HE COMES OFF THE BOARD TOO.** **His other acceptances for that window close.**

> ⚠️ **"She's probably sending a bunch of profiles as well. So if she is accepted, she comes
> off the board too."**

## 2.3 — ⚠️⚠️ EVERYONE ELSE IS TOLD

> ⚠️ **"We let everybody else know. It's a courtesy."**

⚠️ **Every party holding a closed offer is notified: the invitation is closed.**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **NOBODY IS LEFT WAITING ON AN OFFER THAT CAN NO LONGER BE ACCEPTED.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **The notice says the offer closed. It says NOTHING about why, about who, or about what was agreed.** |
| 3 | ⚠️⚠️ **A CLOSURE NOTICE THAT NAMES THE WINNER PUBLISHES WHO IS MEETING WHOM.** |
