# 1 — ⚠️⚠️ NO SCAN, NO REVIEW

⚠️ **The encounter completes. VAI-CHECK ran at the door, so a review window opens.**

⚠️⚠️ **THAT IS WHAT STOPS A REVIEW BEING WRITTEN ABOUT A MEETING THAT NEVER TOOK PLACE.**

## 1.1 — ⚠️ The clock

⚠️ **48 hours. Both parties. The same clock.**

⚠️⚠️ **IT STARTS AT THE FIRST EVENT SCAN. A RE-CHECK DOES NOT RESTART IT** — otherwise the
window is extendable at will.

## 1.2 — ⚠️⚠️ THE ONE EXCEPTION — A CONFIRMED NO-SHOW

**Nobody scanned, so nothing opened. Then a no-show is declared and confirmed.**

> ⚠️⚠️ **THE PERSON WHO WAS THERE CAN LEAVE A REVIEW. THE PERSON WHO DID NOT TURN UP
> CANNOT.**

⚠️ **One gate, not two.** **They wasted someone's evening, so they do not get to describe
it.** — `CANON-06` §8.6

# 2 — ⚠️⚠️ THE SEALED PAIR

## 2.1 — The mechanic

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️ **Each writes without seeing the other's.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **NEITHER KNOWS WHAT WAS SAID UNTIL BOTH HAVE SAID IT.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **Both submit → both publish at once.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **Only one submits → it publishes at 48 hours and the other loses their turn.** |
| 5 | ⚠️ **Neither submits → nothing publishes. Silently.** |

## 2.2 — ⚠️⚠️ WHY THIS IS THE WHOLE THING

> ⚠️⚠️ **SHE CANNOT RETALIATE AGAINST A REVIEW SHE HAS NOT SEEN. THAT IS THE ENTIRE REASON
> THE REVIEWS MEAN ANYTHING.**

⚠️ **An open system produces mutual five stars, because both sides are managing the other's
response.** ⚠️⚠️ **A SEALED PAIR PRODUCES WHAT HAPPENED.**

## 2.3 — ⚠️ Permanent

⚠️⚠️ **PUBLISHED REVIEWS ARE PERMANENT. NOBODY DELETES THEIR WAY OUT OF A REPUTATION.**

❌ **No edits. No replies. No deletion. Ever.**

# 3 — ⚠️⚠️ WHAT A REVIEW CONTAINS

## 3.1 — Five criteria per side, four shared. 1–5 stars each.

| ⚠️ **CLIENT RATES THE PROVIDER** | ⚠️ **PROVIDER RATES THE CLIENT** |
|---|---|
| **Punctuality** | **Punctuality** |
| **Attitude** | **Attitude** |
| **Communication** | **Communication** |
| **Hygiene** | **Hygiene** |
| ⚠️ **Experience** | ⚠️⚠️ **FINANCIAL COMPLIANCE** |

⚠️ **The overall is the average of the five.**

## 3.2 — ⚠️⚠️ FINANCIAL COMPLIANCE

⚠️ **It measures whether he paid what he agreed.**

⚠️⚠️ **IT IS THE SINGLE MOST CONSEQUENTIAL FIELD ON THE FORM, AND THE LABEL MUST SAY WHAT IT
MEASURES RATHER THAN HIDING BEHIND "FINANCIAL."**

## 3.3 — ⚠️ Respectful was dropped

⚠️ **It overlapped with Attitude.** **Two fields measuring one thing produce two ratings that
disagree.**

## 3.4 — ⚠️⚠️ THE WRITTEN COMMENT — 300 CHARACTERS, BOTH SIDES

> ⚠️⚠️ **THE CHARACTER LIMIT IS A PRIVACY CONTROL WEARING A FORMATTING COSTUME.**

⚠️ **A long box invites a story, and a story invites detail — where she works, what he looks
like, what happened in the room.**

⚠️ **A short box produces *"Showed up on time, respectful, paid as agreed"* and stops.**

⚠️⚠️ **NEITHER SIDE GETS MORE ROOM TO CHARACTERISE THE OTHER.**

## 3.5 — ⚠️ Display

⚠️⚠️ **V.A.I. NUMBERS ONLY.** ❌ **No names, no handles, no avatars.**

# 4 — ⚠️ DISPUTING A REVIEW

```
1  ⚠️ A BUTTON ON THE REVIEW              the window is a setting
        ↓
2  ⚠️ SHE FILLS IN WHAT IS WRONG          her words, with evidence upload
        ↓
3  ⚠️ THE OTHER PARTY IS TOLD             same form, same window
        ↓
4  ⚠️⚠️ THE WINDOW CLOSES                 ⚠️ THE CASE FREEZES
        ↓                                 no more evidence, no edits
5  ⚠️⚠️ FIVE MEMBERS DRAWN AT RANDOM       from the first three groups. Rotating.
        ↓
6  ⚠️ THEY SEE THE CASE                   §4.1
        ↓
7  ⚠️ MAJORITY WINS
        ↓
8  ⚠️ THE RULING IS A COUNT
```

## 4.1 — ⚠️⚠️ WHAT THE PANEL SEES

⚠️ **Party A and Party B.**

❌ **No V.A.I. numbers. No scores. No history. No badges.**

⚠️⚠️ **THEY JUDGE THE CASE, NOT THE PEOPLE.**

## 4.2 — ⚠️⚠️ ADMIN NEVER JUDGES

⚠️ **Not as a tiebreak. Not on appeal. Never.**

## 4.3 — ⚠️ Nobody is named

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **PANELLISTS ARE NEVER NAMED TO THE PARTIES.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **PARTIES ARE NEVER NAMED TO THE PANEL.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **The ruling is a count.** ❌ **Not a written judgement, not a reason.** |

## 4.4 — ⚠️ Panel membership appears on no landing surface

⚠️⚠️ **BEING DRAWN FOR A CASE IS THE ONE FACT A DISPUTING PARTY WOULD WANT, AND IT IS WHAT
MAKES A PANELLIST REACHABLE.**

⚠️ **It lives inside the floor, behind a tap. Never on a home screen, never on a lock screen,
never in a push.**
