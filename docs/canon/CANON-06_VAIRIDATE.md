# 1 — ⚠️ WHAT IT IS AND IS NOT

⚠️⚠️ **VAIRIDATE IS NOT A DESTINATION.** ❌ **No tab, no icon, no entry in either person's
navigation.**

⚠️ **It is a button on one screen and a row in a queue on another.**

> ⚠️ **He did not open a feature. He pressed a thing on a page.**

⚠️⚠️ **SHE DOES NOT RECEIVE "A VAIRIDATE." SHE RECEIVES A REQUEST.** **The request does not
carry the name of the button that made it.**

## 1.1 — ⚠️⚠️ IT IS THE ONLY PROTOCOL WITH AN ADDRESS

| Protocol | Location |
|---|---|
| **Available Now** | **A radius** |
| **Invitations** | **Roughly where** |
| **The feed** | **A city at most** |
| ⚠️⚠️ **VAIRIDATE** | ⚠️⚠️ **AN ADDRESS, DECLARED BY WHOEVER HOSTS** |

⚠️ **Incall the address is hers. Outcall it is his.** ⚠️⚠️ **IT MAY ARRIVE LATE, BUT THERE IS
NO ACCEPTANCE WITHOUT IT.**

⚠️⚠️ **THAT IS WHY THE NO-SHOW CAN ONLY EXIST HERE.** **Everywhere else there is no agreed
place to compare a position against.** — §8

# 2 — ⚠️⚠️ THE TIER LINE

> ⚠️⚠️ **"PLUS LETS YOU REQUEST A VAIRIDATE. PREMIUM LETS YOU TAKE THEM."**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **A provider initiates nothing.** | **Vairidate is client-initiated in all four origins, which is why it sits at Plus.** |
| ⚠️ **Premium plus a calendar is what makes her reachable.** | ⚠️ **Of 10,000 escorts, roughly 7,000 are Free or Plus and 3,000 are Premium with a Vairidate set up.** |

⚠️⚠️ **THIS IS A CAPABILITY STATEMENT, NEVER A RESTRICTION.** **A Plus provider is not
blocked from something she paid for — she simply does not appear as bookable.**

⚠️⚠️ **SHE MUST NEVER DISCOVER THIS FROM SILENCE.** **A Plus provider with no calendar has a
profile that states she is not accepting Vairidates, rather than a row that leads nowhere.**

**It appears in three places, always in the positive form:** the tier card at the moment of
choosing · onboarding · the calendar empty state.

not need to.** ⚠️⚠️ **DRAW ONE REQUEST CARD, NOT FOUR.**

⚠️ **The origin badge is display only. It changes nothing about what happens next.**

⚠️⚠️ **SPEC THE REQUEST OBJECT ONCE. THE FOUR BUTTONS ARE TRIVIAL.**

# 4 — ⚠️⚠️ A REQUEST IS NOT AN EVENT

> ⚠️⚠️ **"THE ONLINE ONLY BECOMES AN EVENT ONCE IT'S COMPLETED." "BOTH PARTIES MUST
> ACCEPT."**

| State | |
|---|---|

| ⚠️ **She accepted, he has not** | ⚠️⚠️ **STILL A REQUEST.** ❌ **Not in the panel. No QR. Nothing booked.** |
| ⚠️⚠️ **BOTH ACCEPTED** | ⚠️⚠️ **AN EVENT. THE CONTRACT IS SIGNED. THE QR EXISTS.** |

⚠️⚠️ **ACCEPTANCE ONLINE IS THE QR SCAN.** **Steps 1 through 3 of the one rule in `CANON-05`
§1 are done before they meet.**

⚠️ **At the door, two things remain: scan the QR as a presence check, and both scan faces.**

# 5 — ⚠️ THE BOOKING FLOW — HE PROPOSES

⚠️ **Keep it short. He is on a profile, interested, and every field is a chance to
reconsider.**

| | | |

⚠️⚠️ **NO ADDRESS IS EXCHANGED HERE.** **Location is given per contact, at the point of

## 5.1 — ⚠️⚠️ HER CANCELLATION POLICY, VERBATIM, BEFORE HE SENDS

⚠️ **This is not a courtesy.**

⚠️⚠️ **THE POLICY IS SNAPSHOTTED AT BOOKING — CAPTURED WORD FOR WORD, TIMESTAMPED,
IMMUTABLE.** **A dispute panel judges against the terms as published when he booked, not her
current ones.**

⚠️⚠️ **A SNAPSHOT OF SOMETHING HE WAS NEVER SHOWN IS INDEFENSIBLE.**

# 6 — ⚠️ THE REQUEST CARD AND THE ACCEPT SCREEN

## 6.1 — The card

**Lands in Notifications with everything else that arrives.**

**On it:** his V.A.I. · when · how long · incall or outcall · his message · ⚠️ **any
disclosure badge**

⚠️⚠️ **THE INBOX EMPTIES.** **She decides and the item leaves.** ❌ **Not a queue that
accumulates.**

## 6.2 — ⚠️⚠️ THE ACCEPT SCREEN — AND WHAT CHANGED

⚠️⚠️ **HISTORY IS NEVER REVEALED BY ANY SCREEN. IT IS ACCESSIBLE AT ANY TIME.** —
`CANON-05` §1.2

⚠️ **The old CANON-06 made this screen load-bearing: it had to show his full review history,
because the door had no reveal.** ⚠️⚠️ **NOTHING DEPENDS ON THAT NOW. SHE LOOKED WHEN SHE
WANTED TO.**

**The screen carries:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **The proposal** | When, how long, incall or outcall |
| **His message** | |
| ⚠️ **Any disclosure badge** | **With the ⓘ and, for a violent offender flag, the route to his explanation** |
| ⚠️ **Her own cancellation policy** | **As he saw it. She should know what she is bound by.** |
| ⚠️ **A route to his history** | ⚠️ **Available, not pushed. She may already have read it.** |

⚠️⚠️ **ACCEPTING SIGNS THE MUTUAL CONSENT CONTRACT.** **Record the version. Record proof of
display.**

**Two actions: accept, or decline.**

# 7 — ⚠️⚠️ THE ADDRESS AND THE LAST MILE

## 7.1 — ⚠️ The host declares it

⚠️ **Incall it is hers. Outcall it is his.** ⚠️⚠️ **THERE IS NO ACCEPTANCE WITHOUT IT.**

⚠️⚠️ **THE ADDRESS IS STORED ON THE AGREEMENT AT ACCEPTANCE.** **The no-show comparison
depends on it.** — §8

## 7.2 — ⚠️ Three release modes, chosen by the host

> ⚠️ **"The escort determines. They don't always want to give their address until they know
> the appointment is coming and close."**

| Mode | |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **RELEASE OUTRIGHT** | **The address is on the agreement and visible from acceptance.** ⚠️⚠️ **THIS IS WHAT SHIPS.** |
| ⬜ **STAGED REVEAL** | **An approximate point to travel to; the real address appears when he is close.** ⚠️ **DEFERRED — §7.3** |
| ⬜ **ADDRESS PLUS ARRIVAL NOTICE** | **He has the address; she is told when he is close.** ⚠️ **DEFERRED — §7.3** |

## 7.3 — ⚠️⚠️ WHY TWO OF THE THREE ARE DEFERRED

⚠️⚠️ **A PWA CANNOT WATCH A POSITION WITH THE APP CLOSED. iOS GIVES IT NOTHING.** **So
nothing crosses a threshold silently.**

> ⚠️ **"I don't want to put anything on it that doesn't work."**

⚠️ **Manual release ships. Proximity modes are specified and wait for native.**

⚠️ **When they do ship, the instruction arrives AT ACCEPTANCE, not at the door:** *keep
Vairify open on the way, your address unlocks when you are close.* ⚠️⚠️ **A MAN DISCOVERING
THAT REQUIREMENT WHILE PARKED OUTSIDE THE WRONG BUILDING IS THE FAILURE CASE.**

⚠️ **And there must always be a manual release she can use at any moment** — a dead phone, a
killed app, denied location.

## 7.4 — ⚠️ Last-mile detail

⚠️ **Separate from the address.** **Gate code, where to park, apartment number.**

⚠️⚠️ **IT IS THE MOST SENSITIVE STRING IN THE PRODUCT** — it says she is there and how to
reach her door. **It appears close to the appointment and it is hers to send.**

# 8 — ⚠️⚠️ THE NO-SHOW

> ⚠️ **The claim is that nobody wastes your time when their reputation is on the line.**
> ⚠️⚠️ **IF A NO-SHOW CARRIES NOTHING, THAT CLAIM IS EMPTY.**

## 8.1 — ⚠️⚠️ IT IS SYMMETRICAL

⚠️⚠️ **PROVIDERS MISS APPOINTMENTS TOO. NOTHING IN THIS IS TIED TO A ROLE.**

## 8.2 — The sequence

```
the appointment time comes · nobody scanned
        ↓
⚠️ THE OFFENDED PARTY PRESSES NO-SHOW, INSIDE THE WINDOW
        ↓
⚠️⚠️ THEIR GPS IS TAKEN FIRST
        ↓        compared to the address on the agreement
        ↓        ⚠️ A CLAIM FROM SOMEONE NOT AT THE ADDRESS IS REFUSED
        ↓          BEFORE THE OTHER PARTY IS TOLD ANYTHING
the other is told: "765UT7X has declared you a no-show"
⚠️ AND IS GIVEN A WAY TO REACH THAT MEMBER
        ↓
   ┌────────────┴────────────┐
   ↓                         ↓
NO RESPONSE              THEY RESPOND
   ↓                         ↓
⚠️ THE NO-SHOW STANDS    ⚠️ THEIR GPS IS TAKEN
   ↓                        against the same address
⚠️⚠️ THE REVIEW GATE OPENS
   FOR THE ONE WHO WAS THERE
```

## 8.3 — ⚠️ The window

⚠️ **Roughly one hour after the appointment time.** ⚠️⚠️ **A SUPER-ADMIN VALUE, NOT A
CONSTANT.**

> ⚠️ **"A no-show declared a day later is a memory. One declared within the hour is a
> position."**

## 8.4 — ⚠️ He is given a way to reach her

⚠️ **Not just an accusation.** **The message names who declared it and opens a route to
respond.**

> ⚠️ **Most no-shows are a delay, a wrong address, or a phone that died.** ⚠️⚠️ **A PERSON
> STANDING OUTSIDE THE WRONG BUILDING IS NOT A TIME-WASTER, AND THE MESSAGE MUST LET HIM SAY
> SO.**

## 8.5 — ⚠️⚠️ IT IS NOT AN ACCUSATION ANYONE HAS TO BE BELIEVED ABOUT

⚠️⚠️ **IT IS A LOCATION COMPARED TO A LOCATION.** **Nobody adjudicates it. The claimant is
at the address or they are not, and the accused answers or they do not.**

## 8.6 — ⚠️⚠️ THE REVIEW GATE

⚠️ **Normally a review window opens because a VAI-CHECK happened.** ⚠️⚠️ **NO SCAN, NO
REVIEW.** **That is what stops a review being written about a meeting that never took
place.**

**Nobody scanned, so nothing opened. Then the no-show is confirmed:**

> ⚠️⚠️ **THE PERSON WHO WAS THERE CAN LEAVE A REVIEW. THE PERSON WHO DID NOT TURN UP
> CANNOT.**

⚠️ **They wasted someone's evening, so they do not get to describe it.**

## 8.7 — ⚠️ Why this matters more than it looks

> ⚠️⚠️ **A SERIAL TIME-WASTER IS THE ONE PROBLEM A REPUTATION SYSTEM CAN SOLVE AND A
> VERIFICATION SYSTEM CANNOT.**

⚠️ **He is a real, verified person. Nothing about the credential stops him wasting three
evenings a week.** **What stops him is that the people he stood up can say so, and the next
person reads it.**

## 8.8 — Seven screens

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️ **The no-show button on the appointment.** **Live only in the window — absent before and after, never greyed.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **Pressing it — the GPS being taken, and what she is told is happening** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **The message the accused receives, with a way to reach that member** |
| 4 | **Their response screen — one action, their GPS taken** |
| 5 | **No response, window closed — the no-show stands** |
| 6 | ⚠️ **Both GPS at the address, neither scanned.** **Someone in the lobby, someone in the flat.** |
| 7 | **A claim from someone not at the address — refused** |

# 9 — ⚠️ CANCELLATION

⚠️⚠️ **EITHER PARTY. IMMEDIATE. UNILATERAL. NO CONFIRMATION REQUIRED.**

> ⚠️⚠️ **REQUIRING THE OTHER SIDE'S AGREEMENT WOULD GIVE HIM LEVERAGE OVER WHETHER SHE CAN
> LEAVE A BOOKING SHE HAS DECIDED AGAINST. SOMEONE WHO WITHHOLDS CONFIRMATION IS EXACTLY THE
> PERSON SHE MOST NEEDS TO GET AWAY FROM.**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️ **`cancelled_at` is recorded.** ⚠️⚠️ **"48 HOURS OUT" VERSUS "20 MINUTES OUT" IS THE SUBSTANCE OF EVERY DEPOSIT ARGUMENT — AND THE ONE FACT VAIRIFY CAN SUPPLY WITH CERTAINTY.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **The money is honour-system.** **Vairify holds nothing. Her policy is a published term, not an enforced rule.** |
| 3 | **Disputes go to the panel.** **He shows the transfer, she has her policy, five members rule.** |
| 4 | ⚠️⚠️ **THE PANEL JUDGES THE SNAPSHOT, NOT HER CURRENT TERMS.** |

⚠️ **A confirmed no-show costs nothing directly.** ⚠️⚠️ **THE CONSEQUENCE IS THE REVIEW
GATE.** — §8.6

⬜ ⚠️ **Disputes currently only exist inside TruRevu, which requires a completed encounter.
A cancelled meeting produces no review and therefore no route to the panel. THAT IS A GAP
AND IT IS NOT BUILT.**

# 10 — ⚠️ THE RESUME PATH

⚠️ **He is mid-request and his own face check fails at home.**

⚠️⚠️ **REMOTE FAILURE HAS NO MANUAL. HE IS TOLD HIS OWN RESULT AND ROUTED TO RE-VERIFY.** —
`CANON-05` §6.3

## 10.1 — ⚠️⚠️ THE PROPOSAL IS SAVED SERVER-SIDE BEFORE THE CAMERA OPENS

⚠️⚠️ **HE IS ABOUT TO LEAVE THE APP ENTIRELY. A PROPOSAL HELD IN LOCAL STATE DOES NOT
SURVIVE THAT.**

⚠️ **Same principle the calendar already establishes — server-side, never local.**

## 10.2 — He comes back

> **Welcome back. You are verified.**
> **You were sending a request to 786TR89.**
> **Tuesday 9pm · 2 hours · Outcall**
> **[ Send it now ] [ Not now ]**

⚠️ **Not a resumed wizard. Not a reconstructed URL.** ⚠️⚠️ **A STORED PROPOSAL HE IS OFFERED
BACK.**

## 10.3 — ⚠️⚠️ THE EDGE THAT MUST BE DRAWN

⚠️ **A requested slot is NOT held while she decides.** **The time he chose can be gone.**

> **Tuesday 9pm is no longer available.**
> **Everything else you chose is still here.**

⚠️⚠️ **HE MUST NOT LOSE SERVICE, DURATION, ADDRESS OR NOTE. ONLY THE TIME IS RE-ASKED.**
