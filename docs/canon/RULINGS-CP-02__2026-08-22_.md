# 1 — ⚠️⚠️ TERMS ACCEPTANCE IS ADMINISTERED BY CHAINPASS, AT REGISTER, FOR EVERYONE

> ⚠️⚠️ **"CHAINPASS IS THE GATEKEEPER MAKING SURE EVERYONE IS COMPLIANT. THAT WOULD BE ITS
> JOB."**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **TERMS ACCEPTANCE MOVES TO THE REGISTER STEP — USERNAME · EMAIL AND/OR PHONE · TERMS. ONE PAGE.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **IT APPLIES TO EVERY MEMBER ON EVERY PLATFORM. NO EXCEPTIONS, NO LEVEL EXEMPT.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **The platform supplies the text. ChainPass displays it, collects the acceptance, and holds the record.** |
| 4 | ⚠️⚠️ **THE MEMBER V.A.I. IS GOOD EVERYWHERE. THE MEMBER CANNOT ENTER A GIVEN PLATFORM WITHOUT HAVING ACCEPTED THAT PLATFORM'S TERMS.** |
| 5 | ⚠️ **Compliance is ChainPass's function. A platform does not administer its own terms acceptance.** |

⚠️ **This supersedes the pay-step placement. The document is needed earlier and universally —
see `BLOCKER-ENROLMENT-TERMS`.**

# 2 — ⚠️⚠️ THE LEVEL IS A GATE, NOT A BUILD

> ⚠️⚠️ **"IT'S EXACTLY THE SAME. WE'RE TAKING AWAY, NOT BUILDING."**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **ONE ENROLMENT FLOW. ONE CREDENTIAL. THE LEVEL DOES NOT CHANGE HOW THE MEMBER ENROLS.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **Common to every level: register · terms · capture · reveal. The member has a credential and the member is in.** |
| 3 | ⚠️⚠️ **PRO ADDS THE PLATFORM'S OWN REQUIREMENTS ON TOP. NOTHING ELSE DIFFERS.** |
| 4 | ⚠️⚠️ **THE PLATFORM SETS THE GATE. A PLUS-ONLY PLATFORM REJECTS AN ACCESS HOLDER AT ITS OWN DOOR — EVERY YES/NO FOR THAT PLATFORM IS SET TO PLUS.** |
| 5 | ⚠️ **Rejection at a platform door is a platform switch, not a ChainPass build.** |
| 6 | ⚠️ **The value of a higher level is what a platform lets the member do inside, not a different enrolment.** |

⚠️⚠️ **DO NOT BUILD SEPARATE FLOWS FOR ACCESS, V.A.I. AND PRO. THERE IS ONE FLOW AND A
SWITCH.**

# 3 — ⚠️⚠️ THE V.A.I. IS ISSUED AT REVEAL. ACTIVATION IS SEPARATE

| # | |
|---|---|

| 5 | ⚠️ **This affects sequence. Reveal is already at step 7 per §2 — confirm nothing downstream assumes the number appears later.** |

| # | |

# 5 — ⚠️⚠️ BRANDING — CHAINPASS BY DEFAULT, THE SKIN IS A SETTING

> ⚠️⚠️ **"ULTIMATELY, WHAT WE'RE TALKING ABOUT IS A SKIN."**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **DEFAULT IS CHAINPASS. EVERY SCREEN, EVERY LEVEL.** ⚠️ **ChainPass mentions the platform with a logo. That is all.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **In substance: *here is your V.A.I. You registered for Vairify in order to qualify.*** |
| 3 | ⚠️⚠️ **ACCESS AND V.A.I. STAY CHAINPASS-BRANDED. THEY BELONG TO CHAINPASS, NOT TO ANY ONE SITE.** ⚠️ **Nobody signs up for a portable credential because of one platform.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **A Pro platform may customise the skin. Offered at platform signup. Opt-in, default off.** |
| 5 | ⚠️⚠️ **ONE SET OF SCREENS. THE SKIN IS A SETTING ON THE PLATFORM AGREEMENT, NEVER A DESIGN FORK.** |

## 5.1 — ⚠️⚠️ THE ONE SCREEN THAT IGNORES THE SKIN

> ⚠️⚠️ **"IF WE DECLARE THAT IT'S VAIRIFY AND IT'S IN A PLACE WHERE SEX WORK IS ILLEGAL, THEN
> THE POLICEMAN COULD CLAIM THAT THE MEMBER DID IT FOR VAIRIFY, NOT FOR CHAINPASS."**

⚠️⚠️ **THE LAW ENFORCEMENT DECLARATION IS ALWAYS CHAINPASS-BRANDED, WHATEVER THE PLATFORM
CHOSE.**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **THE MEMBER IS DECLARING TO CHAINPASS — A NEUTRAL IDENTITY PROVIDER — NEVER TO THE PLATFORM.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **A PLATFORM-BRANDED DECLARATION LETS AN OFFICER ARGUE THE MEMBER DECLARED TO THE PLATFORM. IN A JURISDICTION WHERE THE WORK IS ILLEGAL, THAT IS THE GAP THE MEMBER NEEDS. IT SOFTENS THE PERJURY EXPOSURE.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **This must be legible on the page and in the record, not only in the schema.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **Vairify sets its own skin off for exactly this reason.** |

⚠️ **Whatever any screen says about who holds the member's data must be true. A page that looks
entirely like the platform while ChainPass stores the record must disclose that. ⚠️⚠️ THAT IS
THE AUDIT PROTECTION — NOT THE LOGO.**

# 6 — ⚠️ THE OTP AUTOFILLS ON MOBILE

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️ **iOS fills from the SMS with no action from the member, if the input is marked as a one-time-code field.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **ANDROID NEEDS AN APP-SPECIFIC HASH ON THE LAST LINE OF THE SMS. THAT IS A SEND-SIDE CHANGE TO THE MESSAGE TEMPLATE, NOT ONLY A SCREEN CHANGE.** |
| 3 | ⚠️⚠️ **THE ANDROID HASH IS PER APP BUILD. IT BREAKS SILENTLY IF THE SIGNING KEY CHANGES.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **Both only work when the SMS lands on the device the member is enrolling on. Desktop and a second phone still type it.** |
| 5 | ⚠️⚠️ **MANUAL ENTRY STAYS UNDERNEATH. AUTOFILL IS NEVER THE ONLY PATH.** |
| 6 | ⚠️⚠️ **EMAIL HAS NO EQUIVALENT.** A magic link is the alternative, but it leaves the app and returns — which matters here because the member is mid-enrolment with a session open. ⬜ **Unruled.** |
| 7 | ⚠️ **Note for later: browser and password-manager autofill of the register fields is worth adding. Not now.** |

# 7 — ⚠️⚠️ THE PRO SHORTFALL — CANON ALREADY RULES IT

**Worked through 22 August against a real scenario. No new ruling; recorded because the flow
is not built and the reasoning was nearly lost.**

**Scenario.** AVChexx is a Pro platform. Its custom requirement is a release consent allowing
the studio to give authorities the member ID on request. The member enrols through AVChexx. The member then
registers on Vairify.

**What happens, per `CANON-CP-01` §11:**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️ **ChainPass compares the member credential's completed requirements against what Vairify requires** — §11. |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **THE MEMBER HOLDS PRO ALREADY. THE MEMBER DOES NOT BUY IT AGAIN.** |
| 3 | ⚠️⚠️ **VAIRIFY'S REQUIREMENTS ARE NOT ON THE MEMBER CREDENTIAL. AVCHEXX NEVER ASKED FOR THEM.** |
| 4 | ⚠️⚠️ **A SHORTFALL IS NEVER A REJECTION. THE ANSWER IS A LIST AND A DESTINATION** — §11.2. |
| 5 | ⚠️ **The member is told which are missing and routed to complete them. Both the member and the platform are notified.** |
| 6 | ⚠️⚠️ **REQUIREMENTS ACCUMULATE ON ONE CREDENTIAL. THE MEMBER NEVER ENROLS TWICE.** AVChexx's release consent stays on it and means nothing to Vairify — each platform checks only its own list. |
| 7 | ⚠️ **When two holders meet, neither learns what the other lacks** — §11.3. |
