# PART ONE — SERVICE VISIBILITY

⚠️⚠️ **RULED 16 AUGUST. THIS SECTION IS THE REASON THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS AND IT IS FIRST FOR
THAT REASON.**

---

# 1 — ⚠️⚠️ THREE VISIBILITY STATES, SET PER SERVICE

> ⚠️ **"Some places are cool. Some places won't."**

| State | ⚠️ Who sees it on the profile |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **PUBLIC** | **Anyone** |
| ⚠️ **V.A.I. ONLY** | ⚠️ **A verified credential holder. A stranger does not.** |
| ⚠️⚠️ **HIDDEN** | ⚠️⚠️ **NOBODY. IT APPEARS ON NO SURFACE.** |

⚠️ **Set per service, not once for all of them.** **She may publish some, restrict some and
hide others.**

---

# 2 — ⚠️⚠️ HIDDEN IS STILL SEARCHABLE

> ⚠️⚠️ **"HIDDEN, THEY'RE STILL SEARCHABLE BY THINGS LIKE VAIRIDATE IN THE BACKGROUND. THEY
> WILL STILL SHOW UP."**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **A HIDDEN SERVICE STILL MATCHES.** **She still appears in a search that filters on it.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **THE MATCHING NEVER STOPS. ONLY THE DISPLAY DOES.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **He sees her in the results. He does not see the service that put her there.** |

⚠️ **Price behaves identically.** ⚠️⚠️ **A HIDDEN PRICE STILL MATCHES A PRICE FILTER.**

> ⚠️ **"A lot of people search with price, so they won't show up in those — but they can have
> them hidden and still show up in search."**

## 2.1 — ⬜ The one consequence, flagged not resolved

⚠️ **He filters on a specific service, she appears, and he cannot see why.**

⚠️⚠️ **SO HE LEARNS SHE OFFERS IT BY THE FACT SHE APPEARED. THAT IS A SOFT DISCLOSURE.**

⬜ **Intended, or should a hidden service exclude her from that filter too? UNRULED.**

---

# 3 — ⚠️⚠️ BLANK IS NOT HIDDEN

⚠️⚠️ **THESE ARE COMPLETELY DIFFERENT AND THE SCREEN MUST NOT LET THEM BE CONFUSED.**

| | ⚠️ Display | ⚠️ Matching |
|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ **HIDDEN** | ❌ **Not shown** | ✅ **STILL MATCHES** |
| ⚠️⚠️ **BLANK** | ❌ **Nothing to show** | ⚠️⚠️ **DOES NOT MATCH. SHE DROPS OUT.** |

## 3.1 — ⚠️⚠️ THE WARNING

> ⚠️ **"They need a warning that blank entries are not eligible for Vairidate."**

⚠️⚠️ **A BLANK ENTRY IS NOT ELIGIBLE FOR VAIRIDATE OR ANY ONLINE SEARCH THAT FILTERS ON IT.
THERE IS NOTHING TO MATCH AGAINST.**

⚠️⚠️ **SHE IS WARNED AT THE POINT OF LEAVING IT BLANK. NOT LATER. NOT IN A SUMMARY. NOT IN
SETTINGS.**

⚠️ **The warning names what she loses, not what she did wrong.**

> ✅ **"Leave this blank and you will not appear in searches that use it, and you cannot be
> booked by Vairidate on it."**
> ❌ **"This field is required."**

## 3.2 — ⚠️ Blank price

⚠️⚠️ **A BLANK PRICE DROPS HER OUT OF EVERY SEARCH THAT USES ONE.** **Same warning, same
moment.**

---

# 4 — ⚠️ WHERE THIS LIVES

⚠️⚠️ **IN THE PROFILE. WHERE SHE ENTERS IT IS WHERE SHE CONTROLS IT.**

❌ **Not a separate privacy screen. Not buried in Settings.**

**Each service row carries three things:**

```
   SERVICE NAME                          [ price ]

   Who can see this        [ Public ▾ ]

   ⚠️ hidden → "Still searchable. Not shown on your profile."
   ⚠️ blank  → "You will not appear in searches that use this."
```

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️ **The visibility control sits ON the row it governs.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **THE CONSEQUENCE IS STATED BENEATH IT, IN PLACE, AS SHE SETS IT.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **A summary of what is public, restricted, hidden and blank sits at the top of the section.** ⚠️⚠️ **SO SHE CAN SEE THE WHOLE SHAPE WITHOUT SCROLLING THIRTY ROWS.** |

# PART ONE-B — POLICIES AND CUSTOM SECTIONS

⚠️⚠️ **SAME AREA. SHE ENTERS THEM WHERE SHE ENTERS EVERYTHING ELSE.**

---

# 4A — ⚠️ STANDARD CATEGORIES

⚠️ **Named slots, offered but never forced.**

| Category | |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **DEPOSITS** | **Whether she takes one, how much, how it is sent** |
| ⚠️⚠️ **CANCELLATION POLICY** | ⚠️ **THE ONE WITH TEETH — §4A.1** |
| **Blackout dates** | **When she is unavailable, in her own words** |
| **Announcements** | **Anything she wants seen right now** |

⚠️⚠️ **SHE DOES NOT HAVE TO USE THEM. A STANDARD CATEGORY SHE LEAVES EMPTY SIMPLY DOES NOT
APPEAR.**

## 4A.1 — ⚠️⚠️ THE CANCELLATION POLICY IS NOT AN ORDINARY FIELD

⚠️ **She writes it in her own words.**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️ **It surfaces as a row on her profile.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **HE READS IT VERBATIM BEFORE HE SENDS A REQUEST.** — `CANON-06` §5.1 |
| 3 | ⚠️⚠️ **IT IS SNAPSHOTTED AT BOOKING — CAPTURED WORD FOR WORD, TIMESTAMPED, IMMUTABLE.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **Editing it does NOT change existing bookings. They keep the text he saw.** |
| 5 | ⚠️⚠️ **A DISPUTE PANEL JUDGES THE SNAPSHOT, NEVER HER CURRENT TERMS.** |

⚠️⚠️ **SO THE EDIT SCREEN MUST SAY SO.** **She is told, at the point of editing, that
bookings already made keep the old text.**

> ✅ **"Changes apply to new bookings. Anyone who has already booked keeps the policy they
> agreed to."**

## 4A.2 — ⚠️ Deposits

⚠️⚠️ **VAIRIFY HOLDS NO MONEY AND ENFORCES NOTHING.** **A deposit is something she arranges
herself.**

⚠️ **The field is a published term, not a mechanism.**

⬜ ⚠️ **The dispute model must not assume a payment record exists.** — `CANON-06` §12.5

---

# 4B — ⚠️⚠️ CUSTOM SECTIONS

> ⚠️ **"Custom — anything they want to know or see."**

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️ **She names it. She writes it.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **As many as she wants.** |
| 3 | ⚠️⚠️ **THEY ORDER WITH EVERY OTHER ROW.** **A custom section is a row like any other and moves in the same list.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **Each one carries the same three visibility states as a service** — public · V.A.I. only · hidden. |

⚠️⚠️ **WHY IT IS NOT A FIXED LIST OF CATEGORIES: SHE KNOWS WHAT HER CLIENTS NEED TO BE TOLD
AND VAIRIFY DOES NOT. A FIXED LIST GUARANTEES A MISSING ONE.**

## 4B.1 — ⚠️ What a custom section is not

❌ **It is not searchable.** ⚠️ **Standard categories can be structured and matched. Free
text cannot, and pretending otherwise produces a filter that misses people.**

❌ **It is not a second cancellation policy.** ⚠️⚠️ **ONLY THE NAMED CANCELLATION POLICY IS
SNAPSHOTTED AND ONLY IT IS JUDGED. A CUSTOM SECTION SAYING "50% DEPOSIT NON-REFUNDABLE"
CARRIES NO WEIGHT AT A PANEL.**

⚠️⚠️ **THE SCREEN MUST SAY THAT, WHERE SHE WRITES IT.**

## 4B.2 — ⬜ Open

⬜ **Is there a character limit on a custom section?** ⚠️ **An unbounded box on a public
profile is where an address or a phone number ends up.**

⬜ **Is custom text moderated, or does it publish immediately?**

## 5.1 — ⚠️⚠️ A FULL PICKER. ONE CONTROL. EVERY SURFACE.

⚠️⚠️ **RULED 16 AUGUST. THIS OVERRULES THE THREE NAMED SETS AND THE COLOURS DESIGN PUT IN.**

> ⚠️ **"They can do a picker. Pick whatever colour you want."**
> ⚠️⚠️ **"THEIR PROFILE IS THEIR WINDOW TO THE WORLD."**

### 5.1a — ⚠️ Built once

⚠️⚠️ **ONE PICKER. ONE STORED PALETTE. EVERY SURFACE READS FROM IT.**

❌ **Not a colour control per feature. Not a second picker for badges.**

⚠️ **She sets it once and everything she owns changes.**

### 5.1b — ⚠️ What she picks

| | |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **BACKGROUND** | **Free** |
| ⚠️ **ACCENT** | **Free** |
| ⚠️⚠️ **TEXT** | ⚠️⚠️ **COMPUTED FOR CONTRAST. NOT PICKED.** |

⚠️ **This is not a limit on her choice.** ⚠️⚠️ **IT IS WHAT STOPS A BLACK-ON-BLACK PROFILE
NOBODY CAN READ, INCLUDING HER.**

### 5.1c — ⚠️⚠️ WHERE IT APPLIES

⚠️ **As many surfaces as it can reach without causing a problem.**

| ✅ Takes her colours |
|---|
| ⚠️ **Her profile** |
| ⚠️ **Her page at `vairify.io/<VAI>`** |
| ⚠️ **The profile badge embed** — updates live when she changes a colour |
| ⚠️ **The trust badge** |
| ⚠️ **Her QR ON SCREEN** — §5.1d |
| ⚠️ **Her card in listings, where the surface allows it** |

### 5.1d — ⚠️⚠️ THE ONE EXCEPTION, AND IT IS NOT A PREFERENCE

| Rendering | |
|---|---|
| ⚠️⚠️ **THE PRINTED QR SHE HANDS SOMEONE** | ⚠️⚠️ **BLACK ON WHITE. ALWAYS.** |
| ⚠️ **The QR on screen** | ⚠️ **Takes her colours** |

⚠️ **Colour reduces the contrast ratio a scanner needs.**

> ⚠️⚠️ **A CODE THAT FAILS AT A DOOR IN BAD LIGHT COSTS HER A REAL THING.**

⚠️ **On screen it is bigger, backlit and scanned close.** ⚠️⚠️ **SAME CODE, TWO RENDERINGS.
THE CONSTRAINT FOLLOWS THE MEDIUM, NOT THE OBJECT.**

### 5.1e — ⚠️ Surfaces

⚠️ **Every asset is authored so it survives its worst rendering.**

| Surface | |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **COLOUR** | **Her palette** |
| ⚠️⚠️ **MONOCHROME** | ⚠️⚠️ **DERIVED AUTOMATICALLY. NOT A THING SHE PICKS.** **A greyscale screenshot, a printed badge, a colour-blind reader.** |
| **Dark ground** | Derived |
| **Light ground** | Derived |

⚠️⚠️ **IF A MARK ONLY WORKS IN COLOUR, IT DOES NOT WORK.**

### 5.1f — ⚠️ The wallpaper

⚠️ **Carries a scrim she cannot remove.** ⚠️⚠️ **A PHOTOGRAPH BEHIND TEXT IS THE ONE PLACE
COMPUTED CONTRAST CANNOT SAVE HER.**

## 5.2 — ⚠️ Customisation is Premium

⚠️ **Client profiles are standard.** ❌ **No palette, no ordering, no wallpaper.**

> ⚠️ **"Clients aren't trying to do all that."**

⚠️⚠️ **SHARING STAYS AVAILABLE TO EVERYONE.**

# 10 — ⚠️ THREE OBJECTS, THREE JOBS

| | |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **THE QR** | **A physical artefact. Printed, handed over.** ⚠️⚠️ **OUT OF HER HANDS THE MOMENT SHE GIVES IT.** |
| ⚠️ **THE PROFILE BADGE** | **An embed on her own site. Colourable, and it updates live when she changes a colour.** |
| ⚠️ **THE TRUST BADGE** | ⚠️ **"Verified by" on someone else's site.** ⚠️⚠️ **CARRIES AN EIGHT-CHARACTER CODE SO IT CAN NEVER BE MISTAKEN FOR A V.A.I.** |

## 10.1 — ⚠️⚠️ THE TRUST BADGE IS SERVED, NOT DOWNLOADED

**`vairify.io/trust/A3X9K2M.svg`**

⚠️⚠️ **A STATIC IMAGE ON SOMEONE'S SITE OUTLIVES THE CREDENTIAL.** **She lapses, and a PNG
keeps asserting she is verified indefinitely.**

⚠️ **Served, it stops asserting the moment it stops being true.** **A not-active credential
renders as something honest.**

| ⚠️ Two costs |
|---|
| **The endpoint stays up forever.** ⚠️ **A broken image is worse than a stale one.** |
| **Every page load is a request.** ⚠️⚠️ **A SOFT SIGNAL OF WHERE HER BADGE IS EMBEDDED. DO NOT LOG IT.** |
