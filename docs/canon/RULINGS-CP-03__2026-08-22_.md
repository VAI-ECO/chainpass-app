# RULINGS-CP-03 — TERMS PLACEMENT, TWO-FRAME BASELINE, CAPS AND RECOVERY CUSTODY

**Owner's spoken word, 22 August 2026. Filed the same session. Seven rulings plus two
addenda ruled after the file was first written.**

⚠️⚠️ **THESE AMEND `CANON-CP-01`. WHERE THIS FILE AND CANON DISAGREE, THESE RULINGS ARE LATER
AND THEY GOVERN. CANON IS CORRECTED AT SOURCE AS A FOLLOW-ON EDIT, NOT BY LEAVING BOTH
LIVE.**

---

# 1 — ⚠️⚠️ TERMS MOVE TO THE ACCEPTANCE PAGE

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **TERMS ACCEPTANCE LEAVES REGISTER. IT RUNS ON THE ACCEPTANCE PAGE — THE PAGE THAT CARRIES THE SECOND CAPTURE — §2 · §14.3.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **Register collects contact (and whatever the platform's collection spec adds). Minimum: a contact plus terms accepted before baseline.** |
| 3 | ⚠️⚠️ **ACCESS AND V.A.I. COLLAPSE THAT PAGE TO ONE — TERMS CHECKBOX AND SECOND CAPTURE ON ONE SCREEN, THEN BASELINE.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **PRO KEEPS THE FULL ORDER — TERMS AND SECOND CAPTURE ON THE ACCEPTANCE PAGE, THEN THE PLATFORM'S REQUIREMENT SCREENS IN SEQUENCE — §4C.** |
| 5 | ⚠️⚠️ **EVERY NEW PLATFORM SERVES ITS TERMS AT CHAINPASS. A PLATFORM DOES NOT ADMINISTER ITS OWN TERMS ACCEPTANCE.** |

⚠️ **This supersedes `RULINGS-CP-02` §1 (terms at register). Register is contact only.**

---

# 2 — ⚠️⚠️ TWO FRAMES BUILD THE BASELINE

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **FRAME ONE AT THE KYC STEP — CAPTURED FROM THE LIVE FEED ALONGSIDE THE PROVIDER. THE PROVIDER MATCH PERCENTAGE IS RECORDED.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **FRAME TWO AT THE ACCEPTANCE PAGE — AFTER THE TERMS CHECKBOX IS CHECKED.** |
| 3 | ⚠️⚠️ **BOTH FRAMES BUILD THE BASELINE. NOT ONE HELD FRAME COMMITTED LATER.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **THE BASELINE COMMITS AFTER EVERY REQUIRED DOCUMENT IS SIGNED — PRO — OR IMMEDIATELY AFTER THE ACCEPTANCE PAGE FOR ACCESS AND V.A.I.** |

⚠️ **This supersedes §2.7 simultaneity-as-one-held-frame language. Two moments, two frames,
one baseline.**

---

# 3 — ⚠️⚠️ REVERSE THE CAP

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **ACCESS AND V.A.I. PLATFORMS MAY REQUIRE UP TO THREE PLATFORM REQUIREMENTS AT THEIR DOOR.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **PRO IS UNCAPPED. NO `settings:pro_custom_requirement_cap`. A PRO PLATFORM MAY ADMINISTER AS MANY CUSTOM REQUIREMENTS AS IT ELECTS.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **Vairify's three — signature agreement, law enforcement declaration, background check — are Vairify's election, not a universal cap.** |

---

# 4 — ⚠️ USERNAME IS NOT CHAINPASS'S REQUIREMENT

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **DELETE CANON §2.3 ITEM 1a. USERNAME IS NOT MANDATED BY CHAINPASS.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **A USERNAME IS COLLECTED ONLY WHEN THE PLATFORM'S COLLECTION SPEC INCLUDES IT. CHAINPASS CARRIES IT; IT DOES NOT REQUIRE IT.** |
| 3 | ⚠️⚠️ **THE CHAINPASS MINIMUM IS A CONTACT PLUS TERMS — TERMS AT THE ACCEPTANCE PAGE.** |

---

# 5 — ⚠️⚠️ SHORTFALL ROUTES. NEVER REJECTION.

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **A MISSING REQUIREMENT IS A SHORTFALL AND A DESTINATION — §11. NEVER A REJECTION AT CHAINPASS.** |
| 2 | ⚠️ **EVERY PLATFORM'S TERMS ARE SERVED AT CHAINPASS — ORIGINATING PLATFORM AT ENROLMENT, ANY OTHER PLATFORM ON FIRST VISIT.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **THE ANSWER IS ALWAYS A LIST AND A ROUTE, NOT A DOOR SLAMMED.** |

---

# 6 — ⚠️ PLATFORM TERMS MUST DISCLOSE COLLECTION

⚠️⚠️ **A PLATFORM'S TERMS MUST DISCLOSE WHAT THAT PLATFORM REQUIRES CHAINPASS TO COLLECT
AND WHAT CHAINPASS DOES WITH IT** — CANON §14.3.

---

# 7 — ⚠️⚠️ RECOVERY TABLES ARE CHAINPASS'S

| # | |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⚠️⚠️ **`security_questions` · `security_question_lockouts` · `security_question_attempts` · `security_question_options` · `recovery_codes` LIVE ON CHAINPASS, NOT VAIRIFY.** |
| 2 | ⚠️⚠️ **`security_question_lockouts.cleared_by` ACCEPTS ONLY `chainpass_reverification` OR NULL — CHECK CONSTRAINT, NOT POLICY.** |
| 3 | ⚠️ **Collected at enrolment step 12 (account security). One custodian. No copy in a platform database.** |
| 4 | ⚠️ **Vairify reads lockout state and drives the re-verification journey; it does not own the rows.** |

⚠️⚠️ **THIS RESOLVES `RULINGS-CP-02` §8 ITEM 5 AND CANON §12 ITEM 11. CUSTODY MOVES;
`CANON-MI-25` §4 AND `CANON-MI-33` ARE FLAGGED — SEE `FLAG-VAIRIFY-RULINGS-CP-03`.**

# 8 — ⚠️⚠️ ADDENDUM — THE BASELINE IS GATED ON THE CHECKBOX

⚠️⚠️ **NO TERMS ACCEPTED, NO SECOND CAPTURE, NO BASELINE.**

⚠️ **She does not have to read them. The box must be checked.**

# 9 — ⚠️⚠️ ADDENDUM — LAW ENFORCEMENT DECLARATION STAYS ITS OWN ACT

⚠️⚠️ **THE LAW ENFORCEMENT DECLARATION HAS ITS OWN AFFIRMATION. IT IS NOT BUNDLED BEHIND
THE TERMS CHECKBOX.**

⚠️ **One face capture may cover both — the terms acceptance page capture and the declaration
signing share one frame — but the LE affirmation is a separate act with its own checkbox
or signature step.**
