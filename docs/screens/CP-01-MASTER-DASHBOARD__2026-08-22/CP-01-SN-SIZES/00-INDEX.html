CANON-CP-01 · the master dashboard — SN-42 to SN-50

Provider only, and provider-hosted regardless of what any platform builds. 9 screens × three sizes = 27 files, four stacked states each — 36 state frames. Platforms as rows, providers as rows, every setting, the failures column with its side-by-side, credentials by state, revenue by platform, the health switch and the reds counter, and an immutable audit log of every admin action. Immutability is the storage, not a permission — no signed agreement version can be altered, including by the provider’s own admin.

Drawn to the official breakpoints: phone below 768 is the default with no query, @media (min-width:768px) is tablet, @media (min-width:1024px) is computer. No fourth. The -T file is drawn at 768 — the low edge of the band, which is where a wide layout actually breaks — and every page carries width:min(100% - 32px, 1200px) so the gutter survives there. These are list surfaces and get their real wide treatment: tables run full width and scroll horizontally on the phone, KPI grids go two across at 768 and four across at 1024. Nothing uses the narrow flow column. Two things here are drawn as gaps rather than screens. SN-45 carries Ruling 6 — fraud found has nowhere to go: a reviewer can see obvious fraud and cannot tell the platform, and what the outcome does is unruled, so the report control is present and inert. SN-50 is a stub for the same reason as SN-41.
