# MASTER DASHBOARD — assets

**Nothing is delivered, and one placeholder is load-bearing.**

**The side-by-side on SN-45 is two hatched 3:4 panels.** The baseline and the last-attempt selfie are real faces held by the provider — **the platform never holds one** — and no sample face may sit in a delivery. The panels mark where they render: together, at size, which is what the ruling requires.

**No score, gauge or match indicator on the review screen.** Staff look for obvious fraud, not a score and not a second opinion on the match. A gauge implies a scale, and a scale implies a number.

**No chart images.** Revenue, reds and cost are tables with reads.

**No platform logos.** Platforms are rows identified by name and state. A logo set would make one client look native to the code, and nothing platform-specific exists in it.
